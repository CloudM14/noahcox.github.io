NASA MINDS – Deployable Aerospace Structure

Designed and tested a deployable aerospace prototype under mass, structural, and reliability constraints.

Problem & Constraints

  • Reliable deployment in constrained volume
  • Mass and structural integrity trade-offs

Engineering Approach

  • CAD-based design and kinematic analysis
  • Material selection through trade studies

Testing & Validation

  • Subsystem testing and failure analysis
  • Iterative refinement based on test results

Results & Lessons Learned

  • Improved deployment reliability
  • Identified future structural optimizations
